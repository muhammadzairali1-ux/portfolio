IAP decline — every number as a chart

Visual companion to the audit workbook. Numbers only, no conclusions. Week of 2 February is excluded everywhere as a known data error; the week of 20 July is a partial week.

Reading notes. Weeks 1–15 June carry a known anomaly: transactions run ~28% above trend while revenue stays flat, so payers, transactions, conversion and ATV are distorted in that window. Builds replace one another rather than running in parallel, so build effects and calendar effects overlap. Where capped and uncapped revenue are both shown, agreement between them means an effect is real and disagreement means it is driven by a few large spenders.
