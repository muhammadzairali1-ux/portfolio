Why This Report Exists
  • The Bottom Line — -$69,230, split almost evenly: Rolling Offers diverting buyers (-$31,819) and a Winter Pass gap (-$30,393).
  • The Fix — Offers stopped, Summer Pass launched. ARPPU stabilizing near baseline.
  • The Hidden Threat — 80% of high-ticket buyers churned entirely — only 27% moved to Rolling Offer. Most of June's user base simply left, offer or no offer.

"Churned" Isn't All Game-Churn
92% of the pre-Rolling-Offer payer base stopped buying — but only 61.3% actually left the game. The other 30.1% still log in, they just stopped paying. Rolling Offer buyers retain as well or better than High-Ticket buyers (D1–D14) — this is a spend-per-visit problem, not purely a loyalty problem.
